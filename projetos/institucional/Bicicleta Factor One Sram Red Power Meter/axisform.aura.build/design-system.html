02 / Colors & Surfaces

The primary and secondary palette values, surfacing tokens, borders, and overlays that form the visual structure.

Orange (Accent)

--orange: #ff5a1f

Primary brand color. Used for accents, indicators, CTA buttons, and high-contrast details.

Orange Soft

--orange-soft: rgba(255,90,31,0.22)

Low-contrast orange highlight. Used for buttons background hover states and cards overlays.

Cream

--cream: #f3f0e8

Primary foreground text and high-contrast card background. Soft, off-white hue.

Muted Cream

--muted: rgba(243,240,232,0.58)

Secondary body text color. Softened to reduce screen contrast and establish visual order.

Background

--bg: #060706

Deep dark, off-black base color. Applied as a background fill for the html page layout.

Panel

--panel: #10110f

Alternative surface background. Utilized for deep-contrast panels and nested card blocks.

Panel 2

--panel-2: #151713

Lighter panel surface color. Utilized for high-contrast card structures and backgrounds.

Blur Spec

Glass Surface

backdrop-filter: blur(18px)

Translucent overlays. Merges the foreground content layer with contextual backdrop glow.

Radial Glow

rgba(255,90,31,0.12)

Soft background lights. Ambient radial spheres positioned to guide scrolling focus.

Border / Divider

--line: rgba(243,240,232,0.10)

Grid lines, input dividers, and card borders. Soft, low contrast separator line.

Noise Texture

opacity: 0.18

Static noise overlay applied on top of the document layout to simulate analog texture.

Grid Veil

background-size: 80px 80px

Architectural grid blueprint pattern overlaid dynamically on background layers.
