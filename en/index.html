05 / ABOUT

20+ years of brands. Now on the web.

I have worked in brand design for over 20 years, especially logos. I learned that an effective brand fits inside a simple shape, and that the shape only works when it communicates clearly.

I have also worked in social media, which taught me to design for short attention: every element has to justify its place.

Today I apply that experience to the digital environment. I take on identity, layout, motion and development, with a single point of contact from brief to launch.

I bring to digital the same rigor I have always applied to brand work: every project is handled with judgment and delivered with care.
